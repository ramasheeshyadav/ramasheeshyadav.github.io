About

A statistician who also builds the database.

I am a Statistician (Research) at the Sitaram Bhartia Institute of Science and Research, New Delhi, where I work on maternal and child nutrition, anaemia and diabetes research. I hold a Master's degree in Statistics from Barkatullah University, Bhopal, and have spent more than seven years in public health research, clinical research and health data management. My work covers the full path of a study: designing the case report form, building the electronic database, cleaning and validating the data, running the analysis and writing it up for publication.

Because I work on both sides, the data structure is planned with the analysis in mind. That means fewer surprises at database lock, cleaner audit trails and results that reproduce from a single do-file.

I am currently pursuing a PhD at Lovely Professional University, Punjab, and am focusing on work as a subject matter expert in biostatistics, clinical data management and monitoring & evaluation, with teams that need reliable methods and clear interpretation.
