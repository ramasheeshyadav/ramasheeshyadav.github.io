Profile

Professional summary

I am a Statistician (Research) at the Sitaram Bhartia Institute of Science and Research, New Delhi, where I have worked since 2022 after contributing to the institute's DEDICOM-II and MATCOBIND research projects. My work spans the full research cycle: designing data collection instruments, building electronic databases, data cleaning and validation, statistical analysis and preparing results for peer-reviewed publication.

Working across both data management and analysis means databases are designed with the statistical analysis plan in mind. The result is cleaner data at database lock, transparent audit trails and analyses that reproduce from documented code.

I hold an M.Sc. in Statistics from Barkatullah University, Bhopal, and am pursuing a PhD at Lovely Professional University, Punjab. I am available for subject matter expert roles, consultancy and research collaboration in biostatistics, clinical data management and monitoring & evaluation.
